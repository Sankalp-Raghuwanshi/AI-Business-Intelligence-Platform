# Seller Fee Policy

The marketplace charges sellers a commission of 12% on the item price
(not including shipping) for most categories. Electronics and appliances
carry a reduced commission of 9% due to lower margins in that category.

A fixed payment processing fee of R$1.00 per order is deducted regardless
of category. Sellers on the "Plus" subscription tier receive a 2
percentage point commission discount in exchange for a monthly
subscription fee, and are prioritized in search ranking.

Payouts to sellers are released 14 days after delivery confirmation, to
allow the return window to pass. If a return is filed within that window,
the payout for that order is held until the return is resolved.
