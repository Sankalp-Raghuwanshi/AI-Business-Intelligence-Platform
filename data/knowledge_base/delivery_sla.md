# Delivery Service Level Agreement

Standard delivery windows are estimated at checkout based on the buyer's
region and the seller's dispatch location, and typically range from 5 to
25 business days depending on distance from the seller's state.

An order is considered delayed if it arrives after the estimated delivery
date shown at checkout. Sellers with a delayed-order rate above 10% in a
given month are subject to a temporary visibility penalty in search
results until the rate improves.

Orders that are not delivered within 30 days of the estimated delivery
date are automatically eligible for a full refund, even without a return
request, under the platform's delivery guarantee.

Same-state deliveries average 3-6 business days. Cross-region deliveries
to the North and Northeast regions take the longest on average due to
longer logistics routes, and are the most common source of delay
complaints.
