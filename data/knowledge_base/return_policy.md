# Return and Refund Policy

Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of receiving an item,
in line with Brazilian consumer protection law (CDC, Article 49) for
purchases made outside a physical store.

To be eligible, the item must be unused, in its original packaging, and
accompanied by the original invoice. Perishable goods, personalized items,
and opened hygiene products are not eligible for return.

Once a return is approved, the refund is issued to the original payment
method within 10 business days of the item being received back at the
seller's warehouse. Shipping costs for returns due to seller error (wrong
item, defective product) are covered by the seller. Shipping costs for
returns due to buyer preference (wrong size, changed mind) are covered by
the buyer.

Sellers who receive a return rate above 8% in a rolling 90-day window are
flagged for a quality review by the marketplace operations team.
