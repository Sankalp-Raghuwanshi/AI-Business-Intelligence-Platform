# Review Score Guidelines

Buyers are invited to rate their order from 1 to 5 stars after delivery is
confirmed. The review survey is sent automatically and remains open for
30 days.

A score of 5 indicates full satisfaction with product quality, delivery
speed, and packaging. A score of 3 typically indicates a product that
matched its description but arrived later than expected or with minor
packaging damage. A score of 1 or 2 usually indicates a wrong, defective,
or significantly delayed item.

Sellers with an average score below 3.5 across their last 100 orders are
flagged for a seller-performance review. Review scores are the single
largest factor in the marketplace's seller ranking algorithm, weighted
more heavily than order volume.

Delivery delay is the most common driver of low review scores across the
platform, followed by product-description mismatches.
